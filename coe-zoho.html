SEC-Zoho collaboration

A partnership for digital learning and industry readiness.

Saveetha Engineering College signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with Zoho Corporation on 12 May 2026 under the Zoho Spark Education Program, marking an important milestone in strengthening industry-academia collaboration.

The partnership aims to bridge the gap between academic learning and industry expectations by providing students with exposure to industry-relevant digital platforms, practical learning opportunities and real-world business applications.

Through this collaboration, students of the Department of Management Studies gain exposure to business applications such as Zoho People, Zoho Recruit, Zoho CRM and Zoho Projects, connecting classroom concepts with practical organisational processes.

This initiative reflects our commitment to developing digitally competent, professionally confident and industry-ready management graduates.

What students learn

Zoho digital learning & application areas.

Three Zoho platforms, each mapped to the management subjects it supports.

A · Human Resource Management

Zoho HRM

Zoho People · Zoho Recruit

Students explore how digital HR applications support modern human resource functions through Zoho People and Zoho Recruit.

Key learning areas

  • Employee information and HR administration
  • Attendance and leave management
  • Recruitment and applicant tracking
  • Employee onboarding
  • HR process automation

Related management areasHRM, Talent Acquisition and HR Analytics

B · Customer Relationship Management

Zoho CRM

Zoho CRM

Students understand how organisations use CRM platforms to manage customer information, sales activities and business relationships.

Key learning areas

  • Customer data management
  • Lead and contact management
  • Sales pipeline management
  • Customer interaction tracking
  • Customer relationship development

Related management areasMarketing, Sales Management and Customer Relationship Management

C · Project Management

Zoho Projects

Zoho Projects

Students gain an understanding of digital project management practices and how technology supports planning, coordination and monitoring of projects.

Key learning areas

  • Project planning and scheduling
  • Task allocation and tracking
  • Team collaboration
  • Project timelines and milestones
  • Progress monitoring and reporting

Related management areasProject Management, Operations and Team Management
