Year at a glance

Jun 2025

Board of Studies, Meeting 1

The Board of Studies met on 25 June 2025 with a member from Anna University, an Associate Dean from LIBA, an alumnus from Petrofac Ltd and a faculty member of Saveetha Engineering College. See the board.

Jul 2025

CMIE research workshop for faculty

Leveraging CMIE for Excellence in Management Research and Pedagogy, with Mr. G. Raghavendran of the Centre for Monitoring Indian Economy (CMIE), held in the FinTech Lab (Room 6651) on 30 July 2025.

Aug 2025

PG inauguration, 2025–2027 batch

The new MBA batch was inaugurated on 28 August 2025, with a guest from Geojit Financial Services Ltd.

Aug 2025

NSE Academy NSmart workshops

Equity & Derivatives Trading Strategies Using NSmart, run by an NSE Academy empanelled trainer: a faculty workshop on 25 August 2025 and a second session on 30 September 2025.

Sep 2025

Intern Poster Pitch

Summer-internship posters presented on 24 September 2025 to 14 industry panellists.

Nov 2025

DRESTIEN '25 management fest

Six days, 3–8 November 2025: a design thinking workshop, business movie screening, Business Premier League, stock market workshop, Best Manager & management games marathon, and Corporate Walk & Shark Tank.

Nov 2025

BSmart Club 2025–26 inaugurated

The BSmart Club was inaugurated on 28 November 2025 with Business Standard.

Feb 2026

NISM capital-markets webinar

Prospects in Capital Markets with NISM Certification, hosted by the department's NISM Lab with NISM on 21 February 2026.

May 2026

People's Circle, the HR Club

The department's HR club, People's Circle, was inaugurated on 9 May 2026 with a speaker from TCS.

May 2026

ICEPBM 2026 international conference

The department's international conference on Emerging Practices in Business and Management, held on 15–16 May 2026, with a valedictory address from Great Lakes Institute of Management.
