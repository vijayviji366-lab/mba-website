FinTech Lab.

Financial technology and live-market learning, with NSE Academy workshops and faculty research training on CMIE databases.

Activities All centres
About the lab

Learning finance on live-market tools.

  • Experiential learning in a simulated trading laboratory powered by NSE, through the NSE Smart Lab (NSmart).
  • NSE Academy workshops on equity and derivatives trading strategies using NSmart, for faculty and students.
  • Live data and reports from CMIE Prowess for management research and teaching.
  • Guest lectures on markets and the Union Budget, held in the lab.
