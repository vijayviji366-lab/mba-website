MBA Programme

An MBA built on practice, technology and industry.

The Master of Business Administration at Saveetha Engineering College (Autonomous) is a two-year, full-time programme over four semesters, with six specialisations and an AI/ML-integrated curriculum. It is AICTE approved and affiliated to Anna University.

Programme overview

The programme at a glance.

The MBA combines academic study with practical exposure, industry interaction, research and technology. Through experiential learning, corporate engagement and professional development, it prepares graduates to work as managers, start businesses and lead responsibly.

Our approach

Five things the programme is built on.

  1. 01

    Experiential Learning

    Case studies, capstone projects, business simulations, live projects, industry visits, research activities and hands-on learning with the NSE Smart Lab and CMIE databases.

  2. 02

    Industry Integration

    Corporate expert sessions, guest lectures, webinars, industry interactions, internships and study tours that connect the classroom to business practice.

  3. 03

    Technology & Innovation

    Exposure to artificial intelligence, business analytics, digital transformation, virtual learning tools, FinTech applications and entrepreneurship initiatives.

  4. 04

    Expert Faculty

    Qualified, experienced faculty committed to teaching excellence, research, innovation, mentoring and professional development.

  5. 05

    Career Development

    Career counselling, placement training, internships, mock interviews, networking events and corporate collaborations.

Saveetha Flexi Learn

Choose your subjects, faculty, schedule and electives.

The MBA is designed to be fully learner-centric: under Saveetha Flexi Learn, students select their subjects, faculty, schedule and domain electives. The brochure lists ten more features of the programme.

  1. A curriculum aligned with industry requirements and integrated with AI/ML.
  2. Experiential learning in a simulated trading laboratory powered by NSE and Harvard Business Review.
  3. Training and certification by Zoho in CRM, HRM, payroll, accounting and more.
  4. Leadership and team-building outbound training by Pegasus Academy and Consulting.
  5. Reflection-based learning with live data and reports from CMIE Prowess, Business Standard and other sources.
  6. Association with industry bodies including NHRD, MMA and the Analytics Society of India.
  7. Field engagements: visits to retail malls, live markets and factories.
  8. Employability-enhancement training and certifications, including Power BI and NISM.
  9. An Innovation and Incubation Centre that supports an in-house start-up ecosystem.
  10. Collaborative teaching with alumni, industry experts and faculty from top business schools.

Source: MBA Brochure 2026, “Unique Features”.

Specialisations

Six specialisations.

The MBA offers specialisations in Marketing, Finance, Human Resources, Operations, Business Analytics and Hospital Management. Each is supported by the department's labs, faculty training and industry activities.

Marketing

Practical marketing and sales skill development in the Sales Lab, with faculty trained on Zoho for Marketing and Customer Relationship Management (CRM). Students also compete in events such as the Ad Mad Show at Drestein.

Sales Lab →

Finance

The FinTech Lab and NISM Lab give exposure to financial markets, investment and financial technology. The Finance Lab has NSE terminals, and an NSE Academy-empanelled trainer has led a workshop on equity and derivatives trading strategies using NSmart.

Finance Lab →

Human Resources

Faculty are trained on Zoho for Human Resource Management. Students take part in the HR Club (People's Circle) and the HR Leaders Conclave, a panel of senior HR leaders from industry.

Meet the faculty →

Operations

Faculty are trained on Zoho for Operations Management and Project Management. Students attend supply-chain sessions such as “Decoding the Modern Supply Chain” (Business Talk Series) and visit factories and live markets.

Meet the faculty →

Business Analytics

The programme's technology strand covers AI, business analytics and digital tools. Students work with live data from CMIE Prowess and Business Standard, can earn the Power BI certification, and benefit from the department's association with the Analytics Society of India.
