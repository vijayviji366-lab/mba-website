About

About Dr. S. Jainab Zareena

Dr. S. Jainab Zareena is an Assistant Professor with 8 years and 7 months of teaching experience in higher education and 6 months of industrial experience. She holds an M.B.A. and a Ph.D., and her areas of specialization are Marketing Management and Human Resource Management. Her research interests include employability of engineering and management graduates, artificial intelligence applications in marketing and human resource management, digital entrepreneurship, work-life balance, mobile payments and cloud computing adoption.

She has authored five Scopus-indexed journal articles, four Scopus-indexed conference proceedings papers, five Scopus-indexed book chapters and 16 peer-reviewed journal publications, with an h-index of 2 in Scopus and 5 in Google Scholar. She holds one granted design patent and has seven patents published.

She has served as a peer reviewer for the International Conference on Modern Management based on Big Data (Beijing, 2024; Hong Kong, 2025), as a reviewer for the Bulletin of Science, Technology, and Society (SAGE), and as Editor of the Journal of Research and Innovation in Technology, Commerce, and Management. She is an Innovation Ambassador of the Ministry of Education's Innovation Cell and received the Sarvepalli Dr. Radhakrishnan Award for Best Faculty in 2024. She has delivered invited sessions on responsible AI, data privacy, intellectual property management, startups and soft skills, and contributes to management education, innovation and the professional development of students.
