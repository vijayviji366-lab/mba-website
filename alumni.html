Our alumni. Our ambassadors.

Graduates who keep coming back to campus.

Alumni of the Department of Management Studies return as speakers, mock-interview panellists, conclave panellists and members of the Board of Studies. This page brings together the alumni network, alumni talks, achievements and engagement activities.

Alumni network

One network since 2006.

The MBA programme has run since the Department of Management Studies was established in 2006. Its graduates have joined recruiters such as Accenture and Valeo, and many stay in touch with the department through talks, mentoring and placement activities.

The department values its alumni as important contributors to its academic and professional ecosystem. Through alumni talks, mentoring initiatives, career guidance sessions and professional interactions, students gain valuable insights from experienced graduates working in diverse sectors.

If you are an alumnus or alumna of the programme, the department would like to hear from you: about your career, about speaking to current students, or about hiring from campus.
