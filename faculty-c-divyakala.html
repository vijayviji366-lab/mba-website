About

About Dr. Divyakala C

Dr. Divyakala C is an accomplished academic and researcher serving as an Assistant Professor in the Department of Management Studies at Saveetha Engineering College. Holding a Ph.D. in Management (2025) and an MBA (2018) from VIT Business School, alongside a B.Com. in Computer Application from Thiruvalluvar University (2016), she combines strong analytical foundations with managerial insight. With a year of postgraduate teaching experience, she instructs MBA courses in HR Analytics, Data Analytics Using Excel, and Operations Management, specializing in Business Analytics, HR Analytics, and Production and Operations Management.

Proficient in both bibliometric and empirical research methodologies, her scholarly interests center on workplace behavior—specifically presenteeism and workplace bullying—alongside psychological well-being, stress management, and gender dynamics. Her research portfolio includes four peer-reviewed publications, notably in Frontiers in Psychology (a Q1 journal with a 2.9 impact factor), and three conference abstracts presented at national and international management forums. In recognition of her academic excellence, she was honored with the Abdul Kalam Research Award in 2024 and the Best Paper Award at the ICFAI Doctoral Conference.

Technically adept in data analysis and visualization, Dr. Divyakala leverages advanced tools such as SPSS, PLS-SEM, AMOS, R, JMP Pro, and Power BI in her empirical investigations. Beyond her core research and teaching, she is an active Life Member of the Commerce and Management Association of India (CMAOI) since 2025. She contributes significantly to institutional and professional development by coordinating academic workshops, such as “The Power of Entrepreneurial Mindset,” and engaging in advanced Faculty Development Programs focused on Artificial Intelligence for Business Transformation and specialized publisher training, establishing herself as a dynamic, data-driven educator and scholar in contemporary management studies.
