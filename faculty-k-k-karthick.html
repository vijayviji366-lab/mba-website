About

About Dr. K. K. Karthick

Dr. K. K. Karthick is an Associate Professor in the Department of Management Studies at Saveetha Engineering College, Chennai, with 25 years of experience in teaching, research and academic leadership in management education. He holds a Ph.D. in Management from Bharathiar University (2009), along with MBA, MHRM, MS and M.Phil. qualifications. Before joining Saveetha Engineering College in June 2024, he served at GRD Institute of Management, Coimbatore, and earlier as Professor and Head of the MBA programme at PMR Institute of Technology and PMR Engineering College.

He teaches Management Concepts and Organisational Behaviour, Strategic HRM, Talent Management and AI in HRM. His areas of interest include human resource management, organisational behaviour, talent management and the application of artificial intelligence in HR. His Scopus profile lists 39 documents with an h-index of 6. He has authored six books, has 52+ patents published, has delivered nine international and more than twenty national keynote addresses, has chaired ten international conferences and has served as external examiner for 18 Ph.D. viva voce examinations.

He is Editor of the International Journal of Marketing & Human Resource Management, Editor and Reviewer of GUIDE: Journal of Guidance and Counseling, and a Reviewer for the International Journal of Computer Theory and Engineering. He is a Life Member of the Commerce and Management Association of India and is certified in Zoho HRMS applications.
