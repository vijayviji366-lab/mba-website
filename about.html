About the Department

Empowering future leaders. Inspiring innovation.

The Department of Management Studies at Saveetha Engineering College (Autonomous), Chennai, has offered the MBA since 2006–07. Here is who we are: our vision, mission, objectives and the outcomes the programme commits to.

Established 2006–07
Introduction

Welcome to the Department of Management Studies.

Established in 2006–07, the Department of Management Studies at Saveetha Engineering College (Autonomous), Chennai, is committed to nurturing competent, innovative, ethical and industry-ready management professionals. Located on the 6th floor of the SEC Complex, the department provides a dynamic, technology-enabled learning environment that prepares students for the evolving challenges of the global business environment and the Industry 5.0 era.

Our MBA programme goes beyond conventional classroom teaching by integrating academic excellence, experiential learning, industry engagement, research, technological innovation and professional development. We empower students to become confident decision-makers, strategic thinkers, entrepreneurs and responsible business leaders.

Specialised facilities including the FinTech Lab and NISM Lab give students practical exposure to financial markets and financial technology, while Zoho-trained faculty bring digital platforms into HR, Operations, Marketing, Project Management and CRM.

Our approach

Learning that goes beyond textbooks and classrooms.

We create opportunities for students to explore, experience, innovate and excel. Students are encouraged to think strategically, solve real-world business problems and develop the confidence to lead in a rapidly changing business environment.

  1. Experiential Learning

    Case studies, capstone projects, business simulations, live projects, industry visits, research activities, and hands-on learning through the NSE Smart Lab and CMIE databases.

  2. Industry Integration

    Corporate expert sessions, guest lectures, webinars, industry interactions, internships and study tours that connect classroom learning with business practice.

  3. Technology and Innovation

    Exposure to Artificial Intelligence, business analytics, digital transformation, virtual learning tools, FinTech applications and entrepreneurship initiatives.

  4. Expert Faculty

    Qualified and experienced faculty members committed to teaching excellence, research, innovation, mentoring and professional development.

  5. Career Development

    Career counselling, placement training, internship opportunities, mock interviews, networking events and corporate collaborations that prepare students for professional success.

Inside the department

Where knowledge meets practice.

The department strongly believes in bridging the gap between academic learning and industry expectations.

Experiential Learning and Industry Engagement

Learning at the department extends beyond textbooks through case studies, business simulations, student presentations, live projects, industry interactions, guest lectures, workshops, seminars, internships and professional development programmes.

Regular interactions with corporate professionals, industry experts, academicians and successful alumni provide students with practical insights into emerging business trends, workplace expectations and career opportunities.

Events, Activities and Professional Development

The department actively organises academic, professional and student development activities that encourage continuous learning and industry awareness. These include Faculty Development Programmes (FDPs), management events, HR conclaves, alumni talks, corporate awareness sessions, placement training, mock interviews and skill development workshops.

Initiatives such as Beyond the MBA, alumni engagement programmes, HR Leaders Conclave, HDFC Bank Fraud Awareness, Business Development sessions and supply chain-related learning activities provide students with valuable exposure to real-world business practices.

Research, Innovation and Academic Excellence

The department fosters a strong research-oriented environment by encouraging faculty members and students to participate in research publications, international conferences, seminars, FDPs, book publications and collaborative academic initiatives.

Through research-driven learning and technology integration, students are encouraged to develop analytical thinking, problem-solving abilities, creativity and innovation.

Alumni Engagement and Career Development

The department values its alumni as important contributors to its academic and professional ecosystem. Through alumni talks, mentoring initiatives, career guidance sessions and professional interactions, students gain valuable insights from experienced graduates working in diverse sectors.

Placement-oriented training, mock interviews, communication development, aptitude preparation, corporate interactions and internship opportunities further support students in preparing for successful professional careers.

State-of-the-art facilities

A technology-enabled learning environment.

The department provides facilities that support academic excellence, innovation, research and holistic student development.

  • Smart classrooms equipped with modern teaching and presentation facilities.
  • Finance Lab with NSE terminals and financial market learning resources.
  • Sales Lab for practical marketing and sales skill development.
  • Life Design Lab for personal and professional development.
  • Innovation Labs to encourage creativity, design thinking and entrepreneurship.
  • Library and digital learning resources.
  • Wi-Fi-enabled learning spaces and student support facilities.
  • Access to recreational facilities, hostels and on-campus cafeteria.
