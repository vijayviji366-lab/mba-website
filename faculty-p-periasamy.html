About

About Dr. P. Periasamy

Dr. P. Periasamy is an Associate Professor of Finance in the Department of Management Studies at Saveetha Engineering College (Autonomous), Chennai, with over 26 years of higher education teaching experience and 4 years of corporate industry experience. He holds a Ph.D. in Management (Finance & Marketing) from Sathyabama University, Chennai (2018), an M.Phil. in Management from Alagappa University (2008), and an M.B.A. in Marketing & Finance from Madurai Kamaraj University (1996). He is a dual UGC-NET qualified academic in Management.

His primary research specializations encompass Financial Management, Commodity Derivatives, FinTech, Algorithmic Trading, AI applications in Finance, Green HRM, and Sustainable Investment Strategies. Dr. Periasamy has over 162 total publications, including 7 Scopus-indexed research papers and book chapters published with international publishers like Routledge (Taylor & Francis), IEEE, CRC Press, and IGI Global. He has authored 4 academic textbooks—including Labour Legislations, Marketing Management, SDGs Global Challenges, and Essentials of Strategic Management—and filed 11 published Indian Patents spanning AI-driven ESG scoring, blockchain microfinance systems, and financial behavior analytics.

He has secured major funded research grants from ICSSR (Rs. 5,00,000 for One Rupee Banking) and CMRG (Rs. 5,20,000 for Water Conservation). Dr. Periasamy serves as NAAC, NBA, and Accreditation Coordinator at Saveetha Engineering College, guides 5 Ph.D. scholars, and has received recognitions including the Best Faculty Award from Shinawatra University (Thailand, 2026), Mahatma Gandhi National Service Award (Ministry of MSME), and National Pride & Excellence Award 2026.
