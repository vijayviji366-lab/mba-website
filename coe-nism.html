About the centre

A bridge between academic learning and the financial services industry.

The Department of MBA has signed a Letter of Understanding (LoU) with NISM (National Institute of Securities Markets), marking a significant milestone in the department's commitment to industry-oriented learning and professional skill development.

The establishment of a well-equipped NISM Lab further strengthens this initiative. The centre is one of the few NISM certification centres in Chennai, offering learners access to industry-recognised, high-demand professional certifications and enhanced employability opportunities.

Overall, the NISM Lab serves as a bridge between academic learning and industry expectations, empowering learners with professional certifications, practical financial market knowledge and career-ready skills for the evolving financial services industry.

The Department of MBA takes pride in this achievement, reinforcing its commitment to experiential learning, industry collaboration and career-focused education.

6 November 2026 · Orientation

NISM Certification Orientation and Examination Registration Meeting

  • 56 participants
  • Academic year 2025-26 / 2026-27

An orientation meeting on NISM Certification Examinations was conducted for MBA learners to create awareness about professional certification opportunities and their relevance to careers in the financial services industry.

The session was addressed by Dr. S. Vasantha, Professor, and Dr. S. Suganya Bharathi, Assistant Professor, Department of MBA, who serve as NISM Coordinators. It introduced learners to the various NISM certification programmes, their industry relevance and the career opportunities associated with professional certifications in the financial sector.

Learners were encouraged to register for NISM certification examinations to strengthen their domain knowledge, acquire industry-recognised credentials and enhance their employability. A structured preparation plan was outlined, guiding the learning process, examination preparation and strategies for completing the certification.

The initiative focused on helping learners take proactive steps towards professional development and improve their readiness for careers in banking, financial services, stock markets, investment management and related sectors.
