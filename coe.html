FinTech · NISM · Zoho

Centres of Excellence for futuristic learning.

Three centres lead hands-on learning in the department: the FinTech Lab, the NISM Lab and Zoho Technology Integration. The MBA Brochure 2026 also lists centres for AI/ML, cloud computing, cybersecurity, AR/VR and game development, business analytics, data science, generative AI, blockchain and big data analytics.

Our centres

FinTech, NISM and Zoho.

Three centres where students work hands-on with financial markets, securities-market certification and the business platforms used in industry.

01
Live markets

FinTech Lab

Financial technology and live-market learning, with NSE Academy workshops and faculty research training on CMIE databases.

  • Experiential learning in a simulated trading laboratory powered by NSE, through the NSE Smart Lab (NSmart).
  • NSE Academy workshops on equity and derivatives trading strategies using NSmart, for faculty (Aug 2025) and students (Sep 2025).
  • Live data and reports from CMIE Prowess, and a CMIE faculty workshop on research and pedagogy (Jul 2025).
  • Guest lecture “Unpacking Budget 2026: Insights for Management Graduates” held in the lab (Feb 2026).

Lab in-chargeDr. M. Ganesan Alias Kanagaraj FinTech Lab In-charge and CMIE Database Coordinator

02
Securities markets

NISM Lab

The department has signed a Letter of Understanding (LoU) with NISM (National Institute of Securities Markets) and set up a well-equipped NISM Lab, one of the few NISM certification centres in Chennai.

  • Industry-recognised, high-demand certifications, with on-campus certification examinations.
  • Knowledge and practical exposure to stock markets, mutual funds, investment analysis, derivatives, portfolio management and financial regulations.
  • Career pathways in banking, stockbroking, wealth management, investment advisory and other financial services.
  • Webinar “Prospects in Capital Markets with NISM Certification” by NISM (Feb 2026).
Latest · 6 Nov 2026 NISM Certification Orientation and Examination Registration Meeting

56 MBA learners were introduced to the NISM certification programmes, their industry relevance and the career opportunities they open, and were given a structured preparation plan for the examinations.

Faculty in-charge · NISM CoordinatorsDr. S. Vasantha, Professor · Dr. S. Suganya Bharathi, Assistant Professor

03
Digital business

Zoho Technology Integration

Zoho-trained faculty bring industry platforms into HR, Operations, Marketing, Project Management and CRM.

  • Training and certification by Zoho in CRM, HRM, payroll, accounting and more.
  • Marketing: faculty trained on Zoho for Marketing and Customer Relationship Management, used alongside the Sales Lab.
  • HR: faculty trained on Zoho for Human Resource Management.
  • Operations: faculty trained on Zoho for Operations Management and Project Management.

Zoho-certified facultyDr. Delecta Jenifer Rajendren (coordinated MoUs and certification programmes with Zoho) · Dr. K. K. Karthick (Zoho HRMS) · Dr. Seema Balan (Zoho HRM) · Dr. P. Shalini (Zoho CRM) · Dr. Catherin T. C. (Zoho Projects)
