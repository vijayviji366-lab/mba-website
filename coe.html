Ten Centres of Excellence

Centres of Excellence for futuristic learning.

The MBA Brochure 2026 lists ten Centres of Excellence: FinTech, AI/ML, cloud computing, cybersecurity, AR/VR and game development, business analytics, data science, generative AI, blockchain and big data analytics.

Centre details

The ten centres.

Each centre is listed in the brochure with the partner whose logo appears beside it.

Finance 01

FinTech

Partner shown in the brochure: NSE.

  • FinTech Lab, Room 6651, on the 6th floor
  • NSE Academy workshops on equity and derivatives trading with NSmart
  • NISM Lab and a pathway to NISM certification
AI / ML 02

AI / ML

Partner shown in the brochure: NVIDIA.

Cloud 03

Cloud Computing

Partner shown in the brochure: AWS and Google Cloud.

Security 04

Cybersecurity

Partner shown in the brochure: NVIDIA.

AR / VR 05

AR / VR / Game Development

Partner shown in the brochure: Unreal Engine and Unity.

Analytics 06

Business Analytics

Partner shown in the brochure: CMIE (Centre for Monitoring Indian Economy).

  • Live data and reports from CMIE Prowess
  • CMIE faculty workshop on research and pedagogy, July 2025
Data 07

Data Science

Partner shown in the brochure: NVIDIA.

GenAI 08

Center for GenAI

Partner shown in the brochure: ChatGPT.

Blockchain 09

Blockchain

Partner shown in the brochure: NVIDIA.

Big Data 10

Big Data Analytics

Partner shown in the brochure: EMC.

Source: MBA Brochure 2026, “Centres of Excellence for Futuristic Learning”. FinTech and Business Analytics details are from the department's documents and event register.
