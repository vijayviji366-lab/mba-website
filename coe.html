FinTech · NISM · Zoho

Centres of Excellence for futuristic learning.

Three centres lead hands-on learning in the department: the FinTech Lab, the NISM Lab and Zoho Technology Integration. The college also has Centres of Excellence for AI/ML, cloud computing, cybersecurity, AR/VR and game development, business analytics, data science, generative AI, blockchain and big data analytics.
