Infrastructure and Facilities

A technology-enabled learning environment.

The department provides facilities that support academic excellence, innovation, research and holistic student development: a Finance Lab, Sales Lab, Life Design Lab, Innovation Labs and smart classrooms, on a Wi-Fi-enabled campus.

Finance Lab Sales Lab Life Design Lab Innovation Labs Classrooms Campus
Finance Lab

Finance Lab, FinTech Lab and NISM Lab.

The Finance Lab has NSE terminals and financial market learning resources. The FinTech Lab and NISM Lab give students practical exposure to financial markets, investment concepts, financial technologies and contemporary business applications.

  • FinTech Lab: Room 6651, on the 6th floor of the SEC Complex.
  • Experiential learning in a simulated trading laboratory powered by NSE.
  • Hands-on learning through the NSE Smart Lab and CMIE databases.
  • NSE Academy workshops on equity and derivatives trading strategies using NSmart, for faculty and students.

Campus

A green, eco-friendly campus.

A conducive learning campus with a built-up area of more than 75,037 sq.m.

Library
More than 44,001 volumes of books, and national and international e-journal subscriptions including IEEE, Elsevier, Springer, ASME and EBSCO Management Collections, plus IEEE-MIT Press e-book collections. Management magazines include Harvard Business Review, MIT Sloan Management Review and DSIJ.
Wi-Fi
A Wi-Fi-enabled campus with 1.5 Gbps internet connectivity, with Wi-Fi-enabled learning spaces and student support facilities.
Learning spaces
Air-conditioned, with interactive panels.
Hostels
Separate air-conditioned hostels for boys and girls, with hygienic vegetarian and non-vegetarian food on all days.
Transport
Air-conditioned transport facility.
Student life
Access to recreational facilities and an on-campus cafeteria.
