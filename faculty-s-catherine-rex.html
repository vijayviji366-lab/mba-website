About

About Dr. S. Catherine Rex

Dr. S. Catherine Rex is an Associate Professor in the Department of Management Studies at Saveetha Engineering College, Thandalam, Chennai. She specializes in Marketing and Finance and has over a decade of experience in higher education, encompassing teaching, research, academic administration and institutional development. She holds a Ph.D. in Management from Sathyabama Institute of Science and Technology, Chennai.

Her research interests include Marketing Management, Consumer Behaviour, Digital Marketing, Social Media Influencer Marketing, Artificial Intelligence in Marketing and Sustainable Business Practices. She has published over 30 research papers in reputed national and international journals indexed in Scopus, Web of Science, ABDC and UGC-CARE, with an H-index of 5, and has contributed several book chapters published by reputed publishers, including IGI Global and other national publishers.

Her academic contributions extend beyond teaching and research to include organizing and convening international conferences, serving on doctoral committees at various universities and strengthening institutional research culture by promoting interdisciplinary collaboration and encouraging quality research publications among faculty and students.

In recognition of her contributions to teaching and research, she received the Excellence in Teaching & Research Integration Award in 2024. Through her academic leadership, research engagement and conference management experience, she continues to contribute to the advancement of management education and research excellence.
