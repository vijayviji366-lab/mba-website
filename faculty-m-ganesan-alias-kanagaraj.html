About

About Dr. M. Ganesan Alias Kanagaraj

Dr. M. Ganesan alias Kanagaraj is an Assistant Professor (Senior Grade) in the Department of Management Studies at Saveetha Engineering College, Chennai, with 17 years of teaching experience at the undergraduate and postgraduate levels. He holds a Ph.D. in Management from Manonmaniam Sundaranar University, Tirunelveli, an M.B.A. in Finance and Human Resource Management from Anna University, and an M.Com. from Alagappa University.

His areas of specialization include Financial Management, Accounting for Management, Security Analysis and Portfolio Management, Corporate Finance, International Trade Finance and Total Quality Management. His research interests include investment analysis, financial markets, sustainable finance, artificial intelligence in finance and business research methods. He has published 22 journal papers, including papers in Scopus-indexed and Web of Science-indexed journals, and has delivered 17 conference presentations. He is the author of two books and holds one published patent.

He has guided three student projects that secured funding from the Tamil Nadu State Council for Science and Technology (TNSCST) and the SRM VEC Student Project Scheme, and serves as an approved MBA project guide for Anna University. As FinTech Lab In-charge and CMIE Database Coordinator, he integrates NSE NSmart simulated trading and CMIE data into classroom teaching. He is a Life Member of the Indian Society for Technical Education (ISTE) and an NPTEL Discipline Star (January–April 2026).
