Internships

Summer internships, reviewed by industry.

MBA students present their summer internship work as posters at the Intern Poster Pitch, where panellists from industry review each poster and question the student.

Intern Poster Pitch, 24 September 2025

Fourteen industry panellists reviewed the summer internship posters. Their organisations included Citibank, Hitachi Energy, Infinite Computer Solutions, Mr. Cooper, MIOT International Hospital and MGM Healthcare.

The brochure describes the MBA as industry-integrated, with strong corporate partnership for 100% internship. The department connects classroom learning with business practice through internships, live projects and corporate interactions.
