About

About Dr. L. Anitha

Dr. L. Anitha is an experienced academician and management educator with over 26 years of teaching and professional experience in the field of Management Studies. She holds an MBA, M.Phil. and Ph.D., demonstrating a strong academic foundation and commitment to lifelong learning, research and professional excellence.

As an Assistant Professor in the Department of Management Studies, her areas of specialization are Finance and Marketing. She is committed to creating an engaging and student-centred learning environment by integrating theoretical knowledge with practical business applications. Her teaching approach focuses on developing students' critical thinking, analytical skills, problem-solving abilities and managerial competencies to meet the challenges of today's dynamic business environment.

Her research interests encompass three major domains: Marketing, Finance and Logistics. In Marketing, her research focuses on consumer behaviour, digital marketing, customer satisfaction, brand management and marketing analytics. In Finance, her research interests include financial management, financial markets, investment behaviour, digital banking, financial technology and financial decision-making. In Logistics, she explores supply chain management, green logistics, warehouse and inventory management, transportation, distribution management and the application of emerging technologies in logistics and supply chain operations.

She has made contributions to academic research through publications in reputed national and international journals, and has authored and contributed to books and book chapters, further enriching the field of management education. Dr. Anitha is a professional member of CMAoI and has demonstrated a strong commitment to research, innovation and intellectual property development, with patents to her credit.

In addition to her teaching and research responsibilities, she serves as the Industrial Visit Coordinator and Outbound Learning Coordinator, facilitating experiential learning opportunities, promoting industry-academia interaction and providing students with valuable exposure to real-world business practices.
