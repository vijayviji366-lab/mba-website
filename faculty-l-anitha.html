About

About Dr. L. Anitha

Dr. L. Anitha is an experienced academician and management educator with over 26 years of teaching and professional experience in the field of Management Studies. She holds an MBA, M.Phil. and Ph.D., reflecting her strong academic foundation and commitment to lifelong learning, research and professional excellence.

As an Assistant Professor in the Department of MBA, Dr. Anitha specializes in Marketing and Finance, the two areas that form the core of her teaching, research and academic contributions. She is dedicated to creating an engaging learning environment that combines theoretical knowledge with practical business applications, enabling students to develop critical thinking, analytical abilities and the managerial competencies required in today's competitive business landscape.

Her research interests encompass emerging trends, technology-driven business practices, consumer behaviour, financial decision-making and sustainable business strategies. She has published nine research papers in reputed national and international journals, has authored and contributed to six books and book chapters, and has eight patents to her credit, bridging academic research with practical and innovative solutions.

Beyond teaching and research, she plays an active role in institutional development and student engagement. She is a professional member of CMAOI and serves as the Industrial Visit and Outbound Learning Coordinator, facilitating experiential learning opportunities and strengthening industry–academia collaboration.

“My teaching philosophy is centered on conceptual clarity, practical application, and student-centered learning. I believe that finance education should not be limited to theoretical understanding but should develop students’ ability to analyse financial information, evaluate alternatives, and make informed managerial decisions.”
