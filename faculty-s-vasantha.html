About

About Dr. S. Vasantha

Dr. S. Vasantha is a Professor in the Department of Management Studies (MBA) at Saveetha Engineering College, Chennai, with over 32 years of distinguished academic experience across premier institutions. Her leadership trajectory includes serving as Head of the MBA Department (Shipping & Logistics) at VISTAS (2018–2023), Assistant Professor at Jaya Engineering College, Academic Coordinator at Magnus School of Business, Head of Department at Valliammal College for Women (8 years), and Lecturer at Chellammal College for Women, reflecting sustained excellence in academic leadership and pedagogy across diverse institutional environments.

Dr. Vasantha holds a Ph.D. in Management Studies from Mother Teresa University, Kodaikanal, an MBA (II University Rank) from St. Mary’s College of Engineering, University of Madras, and M.Phil. and M.Com. degrees from the esteemed Ethiraj College for Women, University of Madras, all achieved with First Class distinctions. Her academic credentials reflect an exceptional breadth of scholarly achievement and an enduring commitment to excellence in management education and research.

A prolific researcher with publications indexed in Scopus and Web of Science, she has guided 27 Ph.D. scholars and 11 M.Phil. scholars to successful completion, and supervised an ICSSR Post-Doctoral Fellow (2019–2021). Her scholarly output includes 10 authored books, 146 Scopus indexed articles and 2 granted patents, complemented by presentations at 95 National and 25 International conferences. Dr. Vasantha has successfully secured competitive research funding from ICSSR, DST Tamil Nadu, NABARD and the Association of Commonwealth Universities (London), and has contributed to a Government STI Hub project with a total outlay exceeding Rs. 2.75 Crore.

Her areas of expertise encompass Financial Management, Investment Analysis, Banking & Financial Services, Supply Chain & Logistics, and Micro-financing for Women Empowerment.
