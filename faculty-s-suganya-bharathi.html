About

About Dr. S. Suganya Bharathi

Dr. S. Suganya Bharathi is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Management Studies at Saveetha Engineering College, Chennai. She holds a Ph.D. in Human Resource Management from SRM Institute of Science and Technology (2025), an MBA in Finance and Human Resource Management from Kongu Engineering College, Anna University, and a B.E. in Electrical and Electronics Engineering from P.S.N.A. College of Engineering and Technology, Anna University, where she was a university rank holder. She has qualified the UGC-NET for Assistant Professor and is pursuing a postdoctoral research programme at Kuala Lumpur University of Science and Technology, Malaysia.

Her teaching experience spans engineering colleges and the School of Law at SRMIST, covering Human Resource Management, Organizational Behaviour, Management Concepts, Entrepreneurship Development, International Finance and Universal Human Values. Her research interests include organizational safety climate, nurses' safety behaviour, grit, workplace stress, emotional intelligence, HR analytics and artificial intelligence applications in management.

She has published five ABDC-listed articles and 9 Scopus-indexed works, including 3 journal articles, 4 IEEE and Springer conference papers, and 2 book chapters with CRC Press and Emerald. She has authored the book Advanced Human Resources Practices (2026) and holds a granted design patent and a published patent. She has served as Chief Editor of the ORBIT-2026 conference proceedings and as a reviewer for journals including the Journal of Human Resource Management and Quality-Access to Success.

Her recognitions include the Research Excellence Award (2024), the Silver Medal at Research Day 2025, the Global Young Achiever Award (2024) and the Best Paper Award (2024). Through teaching, research, accreditation work (NBA Criterion 3 and NAAC) and mentoring, she contributes to management education and the professional development of students.
