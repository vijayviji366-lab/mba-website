Industry Faculty

Leaders who teach.

Beyond full-time faculty, senior industry leaders teach dedicated modules each semester — one-third of the MBA syllabus at Saveetha Engineering College is delivered by corporate practitioners from leading Indian and global firms.

Mr. Umesh Phadke · L'Oreal Indonesia

President Director, L'Oreal Indonesia — chief guest and visiting speaker at Saveetha MBA, sharing senior consumer-goods leadership insights with students.

Dr. Aruni Velalakan · Heidelberg University

Medizinische Expertise, Heidelberg University, Germany — visiting expert, engaging students on experiential learning and global business thinking.

Mr. Rob Peck · O3M Marketing

Director of Client Services, O3M Marketing — visiting industry expert delivering practical insights on client-side marketing leadership.

Mr. Sivaraman Srinivasan · National Bank of Oman

Head – Trade Services, National Bank of Oman — guest speaker at Department events, engaging students on global banking and trade services.

Pegasus Academy & Consulting

Delivers the industry-focused Outbound Leadership Training programme — India's premier outbound training institute, an official partner of the Saveetha MBA.

Industry Body Networks

Student-body affiliations with NHRD (National HRD Network), MMA (Madras Management Association), and Analytics Society of India — CXO speakers drawn from these networks year-round.
