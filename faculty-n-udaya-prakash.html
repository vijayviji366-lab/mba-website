About

About Dr. N. Udaya Prakash

Dr. N. Udaya Prakash is an Assistant Professor at Saveetha Engineering College with nearly two decades of teaching and research experience in Statistics, Data Analytics and Machine Learning. He holds a Ph.D. in Statistics from Bharathiar University, with his doctoral work focused on enhancing the robustness of Support Vector Machines through the integration of Kernel Functions and Depth-based concepts. He also holds an M.Phil. and M.Sc. in Statistics from the University of Madras and Presidency College, Chennai.

His teaching expertise spans Business Analytics, Predictive Analytics, Data Analysis using Python and R, Machine Learning, Operations Research, Probability & Statistics and Data Visualization. He has delivered courses at premier institutions including Great Lakes Institute of Management (PGDM / PGPM) and GKM Engineering College.

In research, Dr. Udaya Prakash has published in IEEE international conferences (ICTBIG 2025) and peer-reviewed journals including IJBSAC, IJSR, IJSRE and IJITEE. His work spans SVM classification, kernel function analysis and applied analytics. He has filed 5 patents (published 2025), covering SVM model validation, sparse graph-driven classification, adaptive kernel selection and predictive analytics.

He has delivered guest lectures at Dwaraka Doss Goverdhan Doss Vaishnav College (Chennai) and KGiSL Institute of Technology (Coimbatore) and has presented at over 15 national and international conferences. He is a Life Member of the Indian Society for Probability and Statistics (ISPS). His research was also featured in ET BrandEquity for a study on household behavior during the COVID-19 crisis.
