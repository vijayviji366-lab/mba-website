Admissions 2027

MBA admissions 2027.

Admissions to the AI/ML-integrated MBA are open. TANCET, CAT, MAT, SAT, GMAT, and XAT scores are accepted. Meritorious TANCET 2027 scorers above 95 percentile are eligible for a 100% tuition waiver plus free hostel, mess and transport, with tiered scholarships below that.

Who can apply

Eligibility requirements.

Graduation

A bachelor's degree (in any discipline) with at least 50% aggregate (45% for reserved categories). Final-year students awaiting results can apply with a conditional offer.

Entrance Test

A valid score in any one of TANCET, CAT, MAT, XAT, CMAT, or GMAT is required. Minimum cut-offs are set each cycle by the Admissions Office.

Work Experience

Not mandatory. Freshers and working professionals are both welcomed. Batch work-experience mix varies by cohort.

English Proficiency

Standard English proficiency is expected. No TOEFL/IELTS needed unless you're applying from outside India.

Scholarships

Scholarships at Saveetha MBA.

There are two routes: Government Quota (TANCET 2027 merit) and Management Quota (10th, 12th & UG marks). Both carry tiered tuition-fee waivers.

Application Process

Six steps. About 10 weeks.

1

Fill the online application

Apply at admissions.saveetha.ac.in. Upload graduation marksheet, entrance score card, and ID. Application fee and waivers are confirmed on the admissions portal.

2

Shortlisting

Our admissions team reviews your profile and invites shortlisted candidates to the next round.

3

Group Discussion (GD)

A Group Discussion on a business or current-affairs topic. Evaluated on structured thinking, team dynamics, and clarity.

4

Personal Interview (PI)

A Personal Interview covering academics, career goals, and work experience if any.

5

Offer & Scholarship

Admission offer and scholarship decisions are sent after the interview round, with a clear acceptance window.

6

Onboarding & Induction

Block your seat with the first-semester fee. Pre-joining materials and on-campus induction dates are confirmed by the Admissions Office.

Frequently asked

15 questions applicants ask us.

If your question isn't here, WhatsApp or call us at +91 8939902737, or email admission@saveetha.ac.in.

What's the difference between Government quota and Management quota?

Government quota seats are filled through the state-conducted TANCET counselling process. Management quota admission at Saveetha Engineering College is on merit, with tuition waivers based on 10th, 12th and UG marks (MBA Brochure 2026). Quality of education, faculty, and placements is the same across both. Exact seat split and quota-wise fees are confirmed by the Admissions Office each cycle.

Which entrance tests are accepted?

TANCET, CAT, MAT, XAT, CMAT, and GMAT scores are accepted. TANCET is the preferred route for the Government quota. Minimum cut-offs are set each cycle by the Admissions Office.

Can final-year undergraduates apply?

Yes. Final-year students can apply with a conditional offer and must submit their final graduation marksheet before the cycle's declared cut-off. After that date the admission is cancelled. Exact submission deadline is published each cycle.

Is work experience required?

No. Work experience is not mandatory. Every batch is a mix of freshers and working professionals.

What specializations can I pick?

Six specialisations: Business Analytics, Finance, Marketing, HR, Operations, and Hospital Management. Track selection happens during the programme based on performance and preference.

Are scholarships stackable?

Scholarship-stacking policy (combining merit, diversity, or need-based waivers) is administered by the Admissions Office. Please contact admission@saveetha.ac.in for the current policy.

Do you offer education loans?

Education loan support is available through the college's banking and NBFC partners. Please contact the Admissions Office for the current list of partners.

What's the hostel situation?

Separate blocks for boys and girls, both on-campus, with 24×7 security, Wi-Fi, mess facilities, and recreation areas. Occupancy is optional; day scholars are equally supported. Exact room types and charges are confirmed by the Hostel Office each year.

What's the placement track record?

A 93% placement record with a ₹14 LPA highest offer, ₹5.36 LPA average salary, and 203+ companies that visited campus, including KPMG, Accenture, TCS, Cognizant, Standard Chartered, HCL, Infosys, and HDFC Life. See the Placements page for the recruiter roster and sector mix.

What's the GD/PI process like?

The GD is typically on a business or current-affairs topic, followed by a Personal Interview covering academics, motivation, and career clarity. Specific panel composition and timing are confirmed by the Admissions Office.

Is the MBA programme AICTE approved?

Saveetha Engineering College is AICTE-approved, NAAC 'A' graded, UGC Autonomous, DSIR SIRO recognised, and affiliated to Anna University. NBA accreditation applies to five undergraduate programmes. MBA-specific accreditation status is confirmed on the institutional accreditation page.

Can I do a summer internship outside Chennai?

Yes. Students intern across Chennai and other metros. Bengaluru, Mumbai, Hyderabad and Pune are common. Specific location split and international partner details are maintained by the Placement Cell.

What's the class composition?

Batch size, academic-background split, gender mix, and state representation vary by cohort and are confirmed by the Admissions Office each cycle.

Can I visit the campus before applying?

Yes, and we encourage it. Book a campus visit via our Contact page and we'll walk you through the NSE Finance Lab, FinTech Lab, hostels, library, and introduce you to current students.

How do I contact the admissions team?

Call +91 8939902737, email admission@saveetha.ac.in, or use the WhatsApp floating button on this site. You can also apply directly at admissions.saveetha.ac.in.
