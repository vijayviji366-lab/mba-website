About

About Dr. Monika Tamilselvan

Dr. Monika Tamilselvan is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Management Studies at Saveetha Engineering College, Chennai with 4 years of teaching experience in higher education and 2 years of industrial experience as an HR recruiter. She holds a Ph.D. in Management from Hindustan Institute of Technology and Science, Chennai, an MBA in Human Resource Management from Pondicherry University, and a B.Tech. in Civil Engineering from Hindustan Institute of Technology and Science. Her doctoral research focused on the psychological needs of Generation Z employees and their impact on turnover intention.

Her areas of specialization include Human Resource Management, Organizational Behavior, Business Communication and training, Entrepreneurship Development, Business Ethics and Human Behavior at Work. Her research interests include Generation Z workforce behavior, intrinsic motivation, job passion, workplace adaptability, employee retention, sustainable human resource management and organizational sustainability. She has published four Scopus-indexed research works, along with peer-reviewed conference proceedings, and has contributed to research in areas including employee motivation, workplace adaptability, career success and sustainable strategies for managing workforce attrition.

She also holds a published Indian patent for the WAP-NG Model: A Future-Fostering Framework for Enhancing Workplace Adaptability and Passion among Gen-Z Employees. Her academic and professional development includes specialized programmes in research methodology, quantitative and qualitative research techniques, SEM modelling, research analytics and Training, Learning and Development through ISTD. With experience in teaching, research, HR recruitment and student mentoring, she is committed to advancing management education, research innovation and students' academic and professional development.
