About

About Dr. R. Divya Ranjani

Dr. R. Divya Ranjani is an Assistant Professor (Senior Grade) in the Department of Management Studies at Saveetha Engineering College, Chennai, with 18 years of teaching experience in higher education. She holds a Ph.D. in Management from AMET University, an MBA in Human Resource Management and Finance from Saveetha Engineering College, Anna University, and a B.E. in Electronics and Communication Engineering from S.A. Engineering College, Anna University.

Her areas of specialization include Human Resource Management, Marketing, Organizational Behaviour, Training and Development, Employee Engagement, and Human Resource Analytics. Her research interests focus on Artificial Intelligence in Management, AI-driven HR practices, employee well-being, sustainable business practices, supply chain management, and women's empowerment.

She has made significant contributions to academic research through publications in national and international journals, including Scopus-indexed journals, and has authored six book chapters, including publications with IGI Global. Her research contributions also include an IEEE Scopus-indexed conference paper and six patent applications in areas such as AI-enabled career development, talent management, brand analytics, and financial decision-making.

She has received several recognitions, including the Teacher Excellence Award 2025, Award of Excellence in Social Service Activity, and Distinguished Young Faculty Award. She is actively involved in academic administration, student mentoring, conference coordination, alumni engagement, faculty development programmes, and community outreach initiatives. Through innovative teaching practices, interdisciplinary research, and academic leadership, she strives to bridge the gap between management education and industry requirements while nurturing students into competent, ethical, and socially responsible professionals.
