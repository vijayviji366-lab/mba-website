About

About Dr. P. Jyothi

Dr. P. Jyothi is an Associate Professor at Saveetha Engineering College, Chennai, with 19 years of teaching experience in higher education. She holds a Ph.D. in Management from Sathyabama University, an MBA from Arulmigu Meenakshi Amman College of Engineering, Anna University, and a B.Sc. from M D Shafi Degree College, S V University. Her areas of specialization are Human Resource Management and Operations Management, and her research interests include Quality of Work Life, Employee Engagement, Glass Ceiling, Operations Strategy, Process Improvement and Inventory Management.

Before joining Saveetha Engineering College in October 2022, she served as an Associate Professor at SRM Institute of Science & Technology, Ramapuram Campus (2009–2022) and as a Lecturer at Sakthimariamman Engineering College (2006–2009). Her research has appeared in Scopus-indexed, Web of Science and UGC CARE-listed journals, and she has contributed book chapters and conference papers on workforce well-being, marketing, finance and AI-enabled manufacturing. Her innovation work includes two published patent applications on AI-enabled workforce productivity tracking and logistics route planning, and one published design patent.

She has guided about 300 MBA projects and partially guided three Ph.D. scholars, and has served as an invited journal reviewer, session chair, keynote speaker and resource person. She has completed NPTEL certifications in HR Analytics and AI in Human Resource Management, and has coordinated admissions, alumni relations, accreditation work (NAAC, NIRF, NBA, AICTE), campus life and women’s grievance redressal.
