Who we are

Welcome to the Department of Management Studies.

Established in 2006–07, the Department of Management Studies at Saveetha Engineering College (Autonomous), Chennai, is committed to nurturing competent, innovative, ethical and industry-ready management professionals.

Our MBA programme goes beyond conventional classroom teaching by integrating academic excellence, experiential learning, industry engagement, research, technological innovation and professional development. We prepare students to become confident decision-makers, strategic thinkers, entrepreneurs and responsible business leaders.

Frequently Asked

Quick answers to what you're probably wondering.

Can't find what you're looking for? Call +91 8939902737 or email admission@saveetha.ac.in

Who is eligible to apply for the MBA programme?

Admission is only on merit. Government quota admission is through TANCET 2026, and Management quota admission is based on 10th, 12th and UG marks. Candidates with a CAT, MAT, SAT, GMAT or XAT score can also avail the merit scholarship on prior approval. For the detailed eligibility criteria, please contact the Admissions Office.

What is the total fee for the MBA programme?

Fees vary by Government and Management quota, with hostel/mess billed separately. For the current year's exact figures, please contact the Admissions Office at admission@saveetha.ac.in or +91 8939902737.

What scholarships are available?

As published in the MBA Brochure 2026. Government quota (TANCET 2026): above the 95th percentile, a 100% tuition fee waiver plus free hostel & mess and transport; 90–95 percentile, 75% plus free hostel or transport; 85–90, 50%; 80–85, 25%. Management quota admission is on merit, with tuition waivers of 100%, 75%, 50% and 25% for 10th, 12th and UG marks above 95%, 90–95%, 80–90% and 75–80%. Candidates with a CAT, MAT, SAT, GMAT or XAT score can also avail the scholarship. All scholarships need prior approval; conditions apply.

Which companies recruit from Saveetha MBA?

The MBA Brochure 2026 reports a 93% placement record, a ₹14 lakh highest salary, a ₹5.36 lakh average salary, and 203+ companies that visited campus — including KPMG, Accenture, TCS, Cognizant, Standard Chartered, HCL, Infosys, HDFC Life and Virtusa.

Is the campus residential? Are hostels available?

Yes. There are separate air-conditioned hostels for boys and girls, with hygienic vegetarian and non-vegetarian food on all days. The campus is Wi-Fi enabled, and an air-conditioned transport facility is available for day scholars.
