Who we are

Welcome to the Department of Management Studies.

Established in 2006–07, the Department of Management Studies at Saveetha Engineering College (Autonomous), Chennai, is committed to nurturing competent, innovative, ethical and industry-ready management professionals.

Our MBA programme goes beyond conventional classroom teaching by integrating academic excellence, experiential learning, industry engagement, research, technological innovation and professional development.

Frequently asked

Questions, answered.

Can't find what you're looking for? Call +91 8939902737 or email admission@saveetha.ac.in
Who is eligible to apply for the MBA programme?

Admission is only on merit. Government quota admission is through TANCET 2026, and Management quota admission is based on 10th, 12th and UG marks. Candidates with a CAT, MAT, SAT, GMAT or XAT score can also avail the merit scholarship on prior approval.

What scholarships are available?

As published in the MBA Brochure 2026: TANCET 2026 scorers above the 95th percentile receive a 100% tuition fee waiver plus free hostel & mess and transport, with tiered waivers below that. All scholarships need prior approval; conditions apply.

Which companies recruit from Saveetha MBA?

The MBA Brochure 2026 reports a 93% placement record and 203+ companies that visited campus, including KPMG, Accenture, TCS, Cognizant, Standard Chartered, HCL, Infosys, HDFC Life and Virtusa.

Is the campus residential? Are hostels available?

Yes. There are separate air-conditioned hostels for boys and girls, and an air-conditioned transport facility for day scholars.

Ready to shape your future in business?

Applications for the 2026 batch are open. TANCET 2026 scorers above 95 percentile are eligible for a 100% tuition waiver plus free hostel, mess and transport. Merit scholarships also available for CAT / MAT / SAT / GMAT / XAT.
